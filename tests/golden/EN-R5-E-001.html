For grown-ups: Keep summaries short: one sentence, no small details.
Read the article. Sum up each paragraph.

1The earliest bicycles, built about two hundred years ago, had no pedals at all. Riders sat on a wooden frame between two wheels and pushed against the ground with their feet.

2Later inventors added pedals to the front wheel. To go faster, they made that wheel bigger and bigger, until some front wheels were taller than an adult. These bikes were speedy, but riders sat so high that a bump in the road could send them flying.

3Finally, designers attached the pedals to a chain that turned the back wheel. Now both wheels could be the same size, and riders sat low and safe. This design worked so well that most bicycles today still follow it.
