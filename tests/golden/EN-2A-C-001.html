The Robin

Mia sees a robin in the maple tree. It has a red chest, and it sings a cheerful song. Mia sits very still and quiet because she wants to listen. After a few minutes, the robin flies down to the grass. It pulls a long worm out of the dirt. Then it carries the worm back to its nest.
