Marco wanted to grow a sunflower for the school garden contest. His sister Ana gave him a tiny seedling in a paper cup. "Be gentle," she warned. "It is fragile, which means it can break very easily."

Marco carried the cup outside with both hands. Just as he set it down, the sprinkler turned on. In seconds, Marco was drenched. He was soaked from his hair to his sneakers, but the seedling was safe.

All summer Marco watered his plant and pulled out weeds. By August, the sunflower was enormous. It was not small like the seedling anymore. It was taller than the garage door!

On the morning of the contest, Marco felt reluctant to dig it up. He did not want to take it away from the bees that visited it every morning. In the end, Ana helped him lift it, roots and all, and they pulled it to school in a wagon. The bees followed them halfway there.
