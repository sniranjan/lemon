## Caption

A holiday gift that lasts all year 🎁

Give a child in your life printable reading and math pages for ages 3 to 12. Pick every level for $99 (it grows with every child in the house), or choose an amount from $10 and let their family pick the levels.

How it works:
1. Pick a gift at paperlessons.com/gift. Add the child's first name and a note if you like.
2. You get a gift card PDF by email. Print it and tuck it in a card, or just send it.
3. They type the code at paperlessons.com/redeem. Codes never expire.
4. They pick their levels and print. Want more than the gift covers? They pay only the rest.

Tap the link in bio and look for "Give a gift".
