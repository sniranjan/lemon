What should a 9th grader know by June? 👀

5 things the Common Core standards (used in most US states) expect by the end of 9th grade, with one to try for each:

1. Factoring (A-SSE.A.2)
2. Quadratics (A-REI.B.4b)
3. Rational exponents (N-RN.A.1)
4. Slope (F-IF.B.6)
5. Polynomials (A-APR.A.1)
