What should a kindergartner know by June? 👀

5 things the Common Core standards (used in most US states) expect by the end of kindergarten, with one to try for each:

1. Count to 100 (K.CC.1)
2. Make 10 (K.OA.4)
3. Adding within 5 (K.OA.5)
4. Compare numbers (K.CC.7)
5. Rhymes (RF.K.2a)

Our Ducklings level is kindergarten work at age 3. Every kid learns at their own pace: start at their level, not their grade.
