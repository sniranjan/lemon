## Caption

What should a 5th grader know by June? 👀

5 things the Common Core standards (used in most US states) expect by the end of 5th grade, with one to try for each:

1. Unlike fractions (5.NF.1)
2. Fraction × fraction (5.NF.4)
3. Decimals (5.NBT.7)
4. Volume (5.MD.5)
5. Parentheses (5.OA.1)

Our Otters level is 5th grade work at age 8. Every kid learns at their own pace: start at their level, not their grade.
