What should a 2nd grader know by June? 👀

5 things the Common Core standards (used in most US states) expect by the end of 2nd grade, with one to try for each:

1. Sums by heart (2.OA.2)
2. Skip counting (2.NBT.2)
3. Adding within 100 (2.NBT.5)
4. Money (2.MD.8)
5. Telling time (2.MD.7)
