What should a 4th grader know by June? 👀

5 things the Common Core standards (used in most US states) expect by the end of 4th grade, with one to try for each:

1. Two-by-two (4.NBT.5)
2. Long division (4.NBT.6)
3. Equal fractions (4.NF.1)
4. Primes (4.OA.4)
5. Decimals (4.NF.6)
