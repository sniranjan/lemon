## Caption

What should a 1st grader know by June? 👀

5 things the Common Core standards (used in most US states) expect by the end of 1st grade, with one to try for each:

1. Adding within 20 (1.OA.6)
2. Facts within 10 (1.OA.6)
3. Count to 120 (1.NBT.1)
4. Tens and ones (1.NBT.2)
5. Letter teams (RF.1.3a)

Our Kittens level is 1st grade work at age 4. Every kid learns at their own pace: start at their level, not their grade.
