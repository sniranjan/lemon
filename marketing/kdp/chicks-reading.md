## Book 1

- Title: **Chicks Reading 1**

- Keywords: preschool workbook ages 3-4 · first words book for toddlers · learn to read preschool ·
  pre k reading workbook · vocabulary workbook toddlers · homeschool preschool curriculum · picture word book for kids

Description:

<b>Big pictures, big words, and a short tip for you at the top of every page.</b>

Chicks Reading 1 is a gentle first reading workbook for ages 3 and 4. Your child meets 200 everyday words (apple,
train, mom, boots) two at a time, and a little "say it again" strip brings back words from a few days before, so
they stick. A few pages a day, with you beside them, about five minutes.

<ul>
<li>100 pages, two new picture words a page</li>
<li>A grown-up tip on every page</li>
<li>Large clear type designed for early readers, with bright original pictures in full color</li>
<li>A progress log and a certificate at the end</li>
</ul>

Next: Chicks Reading 2 (Find It and Tiny Stories). Chicks is the first of thirteen paperlessons levels for ages 3 to
12, reading and math. Free preview pages of every level at paperlessons.com.

## Book 2

- Title: **Chicks Reading 2**
- Subtitle: Find It and Tiny Stories: A Preschool Reading Workbook for Ages 3–4, 100 Pages, a Few Pages a Day
- Keywords: preschool reading workbook · first sentences for kids · learn to read preschool ·
  pre k reading workbook · early reader workbook age 4 · homeschool preschool curriculum · sight words for toddlers

Description:

<b>First real sentences, built only from words your child knows.</b>

Chicks Reading 2 picks up where book 1 ends. The words your child learned come back in a picture game (find the
dog, find the cup), then in tiny stories you read together: "Look at the rainbow. I hug my baby." A few pages a day,
about five minutes, with a short tip for you at the top of every page.

<ul>
<li>Find It (pages 101–140): four known pictures a page to find and name</li>
<li>Tiny Stories (pages 141–200): two to four short sentences a page</li>
<li>Bright original pictures in full color, large clear type</li>
<li>A progress log and a certificate at the end</li>
</ul>

Starts after Chicks Reading 1. Next: Ducklings. Thirteen paperlessons levels for ages 3 to 12, reading and math.
