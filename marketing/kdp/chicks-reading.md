## Description (paste into the HTML box)

<b>Big pictures, big words, and a short tip for you at the top of every page.</b>

Chicks Reading is a gentle first reading workbook for ages 3 and 4. Your child meets 200 everyday words (apple,
train, mom, boots) two at a time, sees each one again and again, then finds them in a picture game and reads them
in tiny stories. A few pages a day, with you beside them, about five minutes.

<b>What's inside</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Picture Words</b> (pages 1–100): two new picture words a page to look at and say together</li>
<li><b>Find It</b> (pages 101–140): four pictures to find and name</li>
<li><b>Tiny Stories</b> (pages 141–200): two to four short sentences built only from words your child knows</li>
<li>A grown-up tip on every page, a progress log and a certificate at the end</li>
</ul>

<b>Made for little hands and short attention spans.</b> One small step at a time, with lots of repetition built
in. Large clear type designed for early readers, and bright, simple original pictures in full color.

Chicks is the first of thirteen paperlessons levels for ages 3 to 12, reading and math. Free preview pages of every
level at paperlessons.com.
