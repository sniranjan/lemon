## Notes

- **New pictures: 40** (none of these slugs exist). One crayon swatch (gray), exempt from the lightness rule.
  Watch the 50–75% fill rule on panda (white/black: use mid-gray body, dark patches as outline-dark),
  plum (purple can go too dark) and the light ones (honey, bagel, apron).
- **Look-alikes with existing pictures, and how to keep them apart:** plum vs cherry/grapes (single fruit
  with a leaf, no stem pair); bush vs tree (no trunk, berries); yawn vs smile/sleep (open "O" mouth,
  arms stretched, eyes open); blow vs bubbles (candle, not a wand); shovel vs bucket (shovel alone);
  melon as a slice so it doesn't read as a ball. No look-alike pairs share a page.
- **Mass / plural:** popcorn and honey are `mass: true` ("I see some popcorn"); neither goes in `plural:`
  ("the popcorn is"). No plural words in this batch.
- **Colors:** orange is already in the bank (the fruit), so it can't be the color word. White is a white
  crayon on white paper and disappears; gray prints well in black and white. Only **gray** is proposed.
- **Story frames:** actions, body, nature, colors and family words use their category frames ("I can paint.",
  "I touch my elbow.", "I love my friend.", "I like gray."). The rest use the default frames, which include
  "Here is my {w}." and "I have {a} {w}." — fine for toys/home/food, odd for hippo, octopus, dolphin, worm
  (same issue as "I have a koala" in STATUS next step 2). No new frames strictly needed; see question 3.
- Card sentences use words the child hasn't met (bamboo, prickly, scoops, fetches); allowed, since the
  card sentence is read *to* the child.

## Questions (with my recommendation)

1. **gray** as the last color word (white rejected, orange taken). Recommend yes.
2. **potty**: very relevant for a 3-year-old, but some parents may prefer "toilet". Recommend potty.
3. **Wild animals in default frames** ("Here is my hippo", "I have an octopus"). Recommend adding an
   `animals` frame group ("I see {a} {w}.", "Look at the {w}.", "This is {a} {w}.", "I like the {w}.")
   together with STATUS next step 2. Either way the words stay.
4. **elbow / tongue**: body pictures are harder to read than objects (like chin/neck). Recommend keep,
   drawn with the same light-tint highlight as chin and neck.
5. **friend** filed under family (so "I love my friend", "I hug my friend"). Recommend yes; no new category.
6. **phone**: kept generic (handset with blank screen, no logo). Recommend keep. Spare words if you
   want to drop any: **vest**, **taco**, **wand**, **kiss**.
