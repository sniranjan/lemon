# Cubs Reading (english 2A): Read Aloud passages (25 passages, 50 pages)

Status: **approved by the orchestrator under user delegation, 2026-11-06** (self-reviewed against the rules
below; the user delegated content approvals on 2026-11-06, see memory `autonomy-content.md`).

Source of truth: `content/passages/english.yaml` (this file is the review record). Each passage is read on two
pages in a row (RF.2.4b, successive readings) with different questions; lengths ramp from ~45 to ~60 words
(read aloud in about a minute). Questions are who/what/where/when/why/how (RL.2.1); every answer is word for word
in the passage, and the child circles it among three choices.

Self-review checklist (all passages): original text; warm everyday topics (animals, family, seasons, play,
helping at home); nothing scary (a fox only comes "near" the rabbit), no brands, no banned words; American
spelling; true to life (bears sleep through winter, lizards eat bugs, crabs walk sideways); pictures are existing
pictures that match the text (tulip red, pony brown, crab for the beach); every sentence passes the sentence-sense
scan (fixes made during review: "First she draws the sun", never "a sun"); wrong choices are clearly wrong.

## 1. The Robin (48 words, pages 151–152, picture: robin)

Mia sees a robin in the tree. The robin has a red chest. It sings a happy song. Mia sits very still and quiet. She wants to listen. The robin flies down to the grass. It finds a worm to eat. Then it flies back to its nest.

- Who sees the robin? **Mia** (not: Dad, the cat)
