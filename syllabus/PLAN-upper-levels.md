# Plan: Foxes → Whales (grades 3–9 work, ages 6–12)

Status: **approved Nov 2026**, with three decisions made after the audit below:
single on-pace ages plus a 13th level (Whales, grade 9, age 12); Puppies and Cubs math compressed into our
own M1/M2 so grade 2 math is covered; the reading sections re-aimed as in the audit table. Nothing below is built yet. Once approved, each level becomes
`syllabus/english/R<g>.yaml` + `syllabus/math/M<g>.yaml` and `ladder.yaml` points at them.

## Ground rules

- **Grade target:** each level matches the US standard for its `content_grade` (CLAUDE.md §15), so
  ages 6–7 get grade 3 work up to ages 11–12 getting grade 8 work.
- **200 pages per strand**, sections in page order, 10-page parts where the numbers step up (as in 3A/2A).
- **Our own internal codes:** `R3`…`R8` (reading) and `M3`…`M8` (math), named after the content grade.
  The current placeholders (`A`, `B` … `G`) can't go in the internal-code ban list, because banning "A"
  as a whole word would fail every sheet with the article "a". `R3`/`M3` are safe to ban. These levels
  also go beyond the original structure on purpose, so their codes should be ours.
- **Answer keys** for every sheet from Foxes on, in a separate `-answers.pdf` as today. Reading
  short answers get a sample answer.
- **Page fit:** child text stays ≥ 20px (§8). Passages up to ~350 words fit on one page with their
  questions. Longer texts (Pandas and up) use a two-page spread: a reading page, then a questions page.
- **Topics fit ages 6–12:** animals, nature, science, inventions, sports, history, everyday life.
  Nothing scary, violent or tied to a brand.

## Audit: is every level really two grades ahead? (checked against Common Core, Nov 2026)

**Rule:** school grade = age on Sept 1 − 5 (a 6-year-old is in grade 1). Target = school grade + 2.
Preschool counts as grade −2 (age 3) and Pre-K as −1 (age 4), so the target is K at 3, grade 1 at 4 and
grade 2 at 5. Codes were checked on the published standards text (thecorestandards.org).

**Problem 1: two-year age bands.** A level with one `content_grade` is two ahead only for the younger
child in a band like "6–7"; for a 7-year-old it's one ahead. Fix: label each level with the one age
a child on pace does it (Foxes 6, Owls 7 … Lions 11). Then 12-year-olds (grade 7, target 9) need a
13th level (Algebra 1 and grade 9 reading), or the site says "ages 3 to 11".

| Level | On-pace age | School grade | Target | What the strands reach today | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicks | 3 | preschool | K | Pre-K: counting to 10, picture words | −1 (starting level, ramps from zero) |
| Ducklings | 3 | preschool | K | K: numbers to 50 (K.CC.1); phrases spoken, not read | ≈ 0 (reading slightly under) |
| Bunnies | 4 | Pre-K | 1 | K: writing numbers (K.CC.3), letters and first sounds | **−1** |
| Kittens | 4 | Pre-K | 1 | K–1: numbers to 120 (1.NBT.1), +1 to +3, digraphs (RF.1.3a), rhymes (RF.K.2a) | ≈ −0.5 |
| Puppies | 5 | K | 2 | Math now 1–2 (built 2026-11-06, our M1: + and − within 20, 2.OA.2); reading 3A being built | ≈ 0 (math fixed) |
| Cubs | 5 | K | 2 | Math now 2 (built 2026-11-06, our M2: place value to 1000, 2- and 3-digit add/subtract, 2.NBT.1–7); reading 2A being built | ≈ 0 (math fixed) |

**Problem 2: a grade 2 math hole.** Nothing before Foxes teaches 2-digit adding and subtracting
with regrouping or place value to 1000 (2.NBT.1–7), yet Foxes opens with 3-digit regrouping (3.NBT.2).
Fix: compress the early math so Puppies = adding + subtraction facts within 20 (2.OA.2) and Cubs = two-digit and
three-digit adding/subtracting with place value (2.NBT.1–7). Same idea for reading: Bunnies and Puppies
each take on the next step's phonics.

Topic names alone don't make reading harder; the text has to be harder too.

**Making it "definitely", not just planned:**
- Every section in the syllabus YAML gets `standards: [3.OA.7, …]`. `ws verify` fails if a
  non-review section's standards are below the level's `content_grade`.
- Every reading passage must score a Flesch-Kincaid grade ≥ `content_grade` (and not far above it).
  `ws verify` checks this for each passage.
- Built (`ws/standards.py`, CLAUDE.md §8 "Two grades ahead"). As enforced: the floor is "no section more
  than one grade below `content_grade`" (not "below"), and passages score within grade − 0.5 … grade + 1.5
  (so the FK floor is half a grade under the target). Cubs Read Aloud passages have no FK range yet
  (a warning).
- The `content_grade` and grade chips on the site come from those tags, so the site can't claim more
  than the sheets teach.

## New sheet types (built level by level, not all at once)

- **Reading:** `passage_questions` (multiple choice + short answer; most reading pages),
  `passage_spread` (two-page text + questions), `word_parts`, `story_order`, `written_response`.
- **Math:** most are one generalized `arithmetic_facts` (operator + operand ranges per part, answers
  recomputed by verify), plus `column_arithmetic`, `long_division`, `fraction_ops`, `decimal_ops`,
  `ratio_table`, `equation_solve`, `coordinate_graph`, `word_problem`.
- Math content is generated from rules with every answer recomputed, so it's cheap. Word problems come
  from ~40 sentence frames per level, with numbers filled in and checked.

## The real cost: reading passages

Every reading page needs original text. A passage can serve 2–3 pages (different question focus),
so each level needs about **70–90 original passages**, roughly **450 across the six levels**, growing
from ~130 words (Foxes) to ~600 words (Lions). Same workflow as the 7A word bank: write in batches of
~20, show them for approval, then generate. Each batch gets a reading-level check (sentence length,
word difficulty) so passages stay on their grade.

## Suggested order

Build one level at a time, bottom up. Ducklings → Kittens follow the existing structure and Ducklings is
the quickest next free preview. Puppies and Cubs need their compressed math (M1: adding and subtracting
facts within 20, 2.OA.2; M2: two- and three-digit adding/subtracting with place value, 2.NBT.1–7)
before Foxes, because Foxes assumes grade 2 math.
